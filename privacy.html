Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

YourFavAlien respects your privacy. This policy explains how this Creator Media Kit handles information.

Information you provide

If you use the partnership inquiry form, we may receive the contact details and message you choose to submit. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential collaborations.

Social-media analytics

With the creator's approval, this site may display selected TikTok and Instagram analytics, such as follower counts, reach, impressions, engagement, and content performance. Account passwords and private access keys are never displayed on the public website.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the Media Kit, respond to partnership requests, maintain approved analytics, and improve the site. It is not sold.

Third-party services

The site may use trusted services to host the website, process partnership forms, and securely connect approved social-media analytics. Those services may handle information under their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, email help@yourfavalien.com.
